### HR Policy Assistant – Usage Disclaimer

**Disclaimer:**  
This HR Policy Assistant is designed to provide general guidance based on the company’s HR policy handbook. The responses generated by the bot are for **informational purposes only** and should not be treated as official HR decisions.

- Always check the **referenced policy section** provided by the bot.
- AI-generated responses should be **reviewed by a human before taking any official action**.
- Do not enter **confidential employee, customer, financial, or personal information** into the bot unless the AI tool is officially approved for such data.
- For sensitive matters such as **harassment complaints, disciplinary issues, compensation, leave exceptions, or other disputes**, contact HR directly.
- If the bot cannot find information in the handbook, **do not assume the answer**; seek clarification from HR.
- Company policy and HR decisions take precedence over any response generated by the bot.
- The bot does not replace **HR professionals, managers, or official company procedures**.

**For any uncertainty or sensitive case, please contact the HR department for confirmation.**
